## Design Specificity Verdict

**Design review:** authored, not templated. The one-colour rule, the 24-seat grid as signature mark, the sticky bar that physically turns SD-gold when M/KD run out, and the departmental structure belong to this argument and no other. Authorship stops at the concept layer: the surface is still generic white cards, pill buttons and soft shadows, the copy captions rather than concludes, and the most prominent button skips the very experience the design exists to deliver.

**Deterministic scan:** 2 findings on the initial and cap states (the same two false positives as before: fallback font name, and a type ratio computed without the clamp() headline), and 25 on the completed state. The new one is real: 24 hits of undersized text, the 10px post labels inside the result tiles, one root cause. Contrast is clean on every text pair except one, the 12px "(minst 12)" qualifier at 3.09:1. Non-text borders on empty meter segments and cards sit under 3:1, which is acceptable where an adjacent filled state carries the meaning.

**Detector-only verification the review could not do:** focus trap, focus return to the opener, inert regions, live region text in all three states, the fixed PM card unfocusable, the font loading over http, and the mobile share button hidden while the result card is in view at both 844 and 667 tall. All confirmed.

**Visual overlays:** none. The Chrome extension is not connected.

## Overall Impression

The argument now lives in the UI state: the chrome adopts SD's colour when the coalition runs out of seats. What is left is a strategic contradiction, not a craft gap. The biggest, darkest button on the page lets a visitor skip the experience of running out of seats, and the result, once reached, can only be shared as a text list rather than as the poster it already is.

## What's Working

1. **The cap state is a real design idea.** The status bar goes gold, empties go dashed gold with "Bara SD kvar". Persuasion by state change rather than copy.
2. **The heavy-departments line** names real people in real ministries. Concrete, checkable, memorable.
3. **Accessibility craft above campaign-site norm,** verified mechanically: inert background, focus trap and return, role=switch, live announcer, reduced motion, preselected SD tab, suggested candidates.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The primary call to action skips the experience.** "Fyll i ett exempel" is the only filled navy button on an empty builder. A first-timer taps it, gets 12 of 24 in one tap, and never feels M and KD running out, which is the entire persuasive mechanism. It stays primary after completion.
Fix: demote it to a ghost button and hide it once six or more posts are filled. Make the first empty card the primary target, or add a primary "Tillsätt ditt första statsråd" that opens the first empty picker.
Suggested command: $impeccable shape

**[P1] Mobile above-the-fold has nothing to do.** Header about 520px plus a 120px bar on an 844px phone; on a 667px phone zero post cards are visible. The hero grid and the meter show the same 24 cells within 60px.
Fix: one-sentence lede, drop the hero grid under 720px, move the L toggle out of the hero into a disclosure, reduce header padding.
Suggested command: $impeccable adapt

**[P1] The cap moment is under-written and loses the numbers.** "Taket är nått – alla lediga poster går till SD" is a system message, and the status line is hidden.
Fix: keep the counts and say the consequence in human terms: "M och KD är slut. De 12 poster som är kvar blir SD." A one-time attention pulse on the newly dashed cards below the fold.
Suggested command: $impeccable clarify

**[P2] The picker is a 90-row phone book.** "Alla" interleaves parties by surname; the SD tab, used twelve times per government, is last.
Fix: "Föreslagna" plus a collapsed "Visa alla 86 namn"; SD first in tab order once fewer than three M/KD slots remain; counts in tabs.
Suggested command: $impeccable distill

**[P2] Share output does not carry the artefact.** The result card is a poster and cannot be exported; a shared link lands on an empty builder.
Fix: render the card to a canvas and share it as an image with text fallback; encode state in the URL hash so a shared link reopens the result while a bare URL still lands on the empty builder.
Suggested command: $impeccable delight

**[P3] Polish defects.** "(minst 12)" at 3.09:1; result tile labels at 10px; toast action wraps to two lines; at 320px the status line truncates; two reset labels.
Suggested command: $impeccable polish

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** one tap on the example and done in three seconds; never learns the rule experientially. The meter's line at 12 is unlabelled. The key rule, "11 platser kvar", is the smallest text in the bar.

**Casey (one-handed mobile):** bottom-sheet controls are thumb-reachable now. But the sheet holds about 90 rows, the cap-state bar takes 120px, and the fixed share button plus a toast can stack in the thumb zone over the last cards.

**Sam (screen reader):** disabled candidate rows carry a description that is never reached because disabled buttons are not focusable; the status banner announces once. The 7s undo toast is a timing dependency with no adjustable alternative except in the completed state.

**Riley (stress tester):** moving a seated person has no undo; overwriting a filled post has no undo; two bulk actions in a row keep one snapshot; the saved-state toast's "Börja om" resets without confirmation and its undo is a second toast.

## Minor Observations

- Example and random always yield exactly 12 SD, so the headline never surprises.
- The single M seat against navy in the hero grid is the weakest pairing on the page.
- The OG image uses circles while the site uses rounded squares; the mark is not yet a mark.
- No "next empty post" affordance on a 3000px page; the sticky bar could carry one.
- "Inga kandidater matchar" has no "Rensa sökning".
- In the cap state "Fyll i ett exempel" stays navy on gold, the strongest button at the moment it is least useful.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the argument is the feeling of running out of M/KD seats, why is the largest, darkest button the one that lets a visitor never feel it?
2. The result card is already a poster. Why does "Dela" hand people a 26-line text file instead of that poster?
3. "Blågul" is the Swedish flag before it is anything else. Does a navy-and-gold page read to a scrolling SD-curious voter as a warning, or as a handsome patriotic government? What in the first viewport tells them which?
