## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** A competent generic admin tool wearing a campaign headline. System font stack, 12px radii, pill buttons, white cards on off-white: nothing in composition, colour or motion says "election week, 12 SD ministers, feel uneasy." The one authored decision (the SD meter stays red at 12/12) is contradicted by the completion banner, painted in the exact gold hex that data.js uses as SD's party colour. The emotional payload is carried entirely by text; the visuals stay neutral and cheerful.

**Deterministic scan:** 28 findings on the rendered DOM (4 on the static file). 23 × low-contrast: `.post-empty` "Tom post — klicka för att välja" at #8b93a3 on white = 3.09:1 (needs 4.5:1), one per empty post. 2 × layout-transition on the two meter bars (`transition: width`). 1 × all-caps-body (department h2s, borderline: label usage). 1 × overused-font "roboto" (false positive: fourth entry in a system stack, no web font loaded). 1 × em-dash advisory (23 of 27 are the same repeated placeholder). Detector-only catches the review missed: nothing new, but it quantified the grey-text problem across all 23 cards. Review-only catches the detector cannot see: white on SD gold at **1.84:1** for `.avatar` initials and `.party-tag`, and #b6893a "sitter just nu på" at 3.17:1.

**Visual overlays:** not available. The Chrome extension is not connected, so no overlay was injected and no live server was started. Evidence came from headless Chrome screenshots and a DevTools-protocol measurement pass instead.

## Overall Impression

The mechanics are honest and the picker is genuinely well built. But the site's one job is to produce a moment of unease at "12 av 24 är SD", and right now that moment is a three-line gold banner that reads like a success confirmation, on a phone where the share button is not even on screen. The single biggest opportunity is the completion state: make it the artefact people screenshot.

## What's Working

1. **The cap bites at the right place.** Disabled rows plus the red blocked message ("…eller så frigör du en annan icke-SD-post först") inside the picker is the one moment the argument is felt rather than read.
2. **"Sitter just nu på: …" and move-on-assign.** Removes an entire class of "why can't I pick her" confusion. Thoughtful mechanic.
3. **The picker itself.** Bottom sheet on phone, centred dialog on desktop, search auto-focused, Escape and backdrop close. Behaves like a native sheet.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Share is invisible on phones.** Under 720px the action row is a hidden-scrollbar horizontal scroller; at 390px only "Slumpa" and "Testa" fit (measured: share button at x=391, viewport 390). The campaign's success metric is shares, and the tool hides its own conversion.
Fix: a fixed bottom CTA on mobile once the government is complete, "Dela: 12 av 24 är SD", with reset demoted to the footer or the picker.
Suggested command: $impeccable adapt

**[P0] The completion state is not a persuasive artefact.** Three lines of gold text, not sticky, toast gone in 2.6 s, no visual of 12-of-24, nothing that invites a screenshot. Peak-end fails; the last impression is a 95-word footer.
Fix: render a result section at completion, directly under the sticky bar: 24 tiles with the 12 SD ones unmistakable, headline "12 av 24 statsråd: Sverigedemokraterna", the SD holders of Justitie/Försvar/Utrikes named, share inside it. Lead the share text with that headline.
Suggested command: $impeccable shape, then $impeccable bolder

**[P1] Gold means three things: brand, SD, and "L is on".** Kicker, toggle-on state, completion banner and SD avatars share #e8b923. The visitor cannot learn "yellow = SD" while yellow also decorates the masthead and celebrates completion. White on that gold is 1.84:1, failing even the 3:1 large-text floor.
Fix: either commit to the "Blågul" concept (gold only ever means SD; toggle white, banner dark red) or give SD a distinct hue. Use dark text (#4a3a05) on SD gold avatars and tags.
Suggested command: $impeccable colorize

**[P1] Destructive bulk actions without warning or undo.** Example, random, and switching L off all overwrite or erase seated ministers silently; only reset confirms, and via native confirm().
Fix: keep a one-step state snapshot and offer "Ångra" in the toast; inline confirm when more than one post is filled; replace confirm() with the site's own sheet.
Suggested command: $impeccable harden

**[P1] Keyboard and screen-reader path is broken at the modal.** No focus trap (Tab escapes behind the backdrop), focus stays on the hidden search field after Escape and never returns to the post card, no aria-live on counts/banner/toast, party tabs lack aria-pressed, disabled rows give no reason. No :focus-visible styling anywhere.
Fix: trap focus, return it on close, aria-live="polite" on the status values and banner, aria-pressed on tabs, aria-describedby from disabled rows to the blocked message, a visible 2px navy focus ring.
Suggested command: $impeccable audit

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** The lede withholds the number ("…ett tak för hur många poster"), and "(hård gräns: minst 12)" is hidden on phone, so "SD-statsråd 0 / 12" means nothing. The L switch is a statement, not a setting, promoted to the hero before any minister is chosen. "Alla" shows 78 rows by first name, twelve of them labelled only "Partistyrelsen". The best entry point, "Testa ett troligt förslag", is the ghost button beside a navy "Slumpa".

**Casey (one-handed mobile):** Share off-screen. Every control under 44px: switch 42×24, party tabs 26px tall (L tab 32×26), close button 23×34, action buttons 33px. Search autofocus pops the keyboard over the list before any name is seen. Toast at bottom:22px sits under the thumb and the iOS home indicator.

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard):** Everything in the P1 accessibility issue, plus contrast failures at #8b93a3 (3.09:1), #b6893a (3.17:1), white on gold (1.84:1), white on L teal (3.07:1). Avatar initials are unlabeled decoration read aloud.

**Riley (stress tester):** Return visit restores a full government while the header still says "Sätt ihop…", no "du har en sparad regering" note. L off with four L ministers seated: four posts vanish with no toast; L on again does not restore them. Random can produce "Björn Söder, Energi- och näringsminister", handing opponents a screenshot of the tool being silly. At 320px the two meters side by side will collide.

## Minor Observations

- SD meter tick at left:50% marks 6 on a 0–12 scale. Either one 24-segment bar with a hard tick at 12, or remove the tick.
- "Slumpa fram kandidater" is the only primary button, so randomising is the loudest thing on the page.
- Candidate sort by first name; Swedish convention is surname.
- Empty-state copy quotes 'slå på "L kom in i riksdagen"' but the actual label reads "Liberalerna tog sig inte in i riksdagen 2026".
- Desktop department grid leaves 50–60% of two-post rows empty; page is left-heavy with no counterweight, and the header has no image, face or flag motif despite the name.
- Footer: one 95-word paragraph in 12.5px grey. Two sentences plus an "Om verktyget" disclosure.
- The 23 "Tom post — klicka för att välja" placeholders drive both the contrast count and the em-dash count. "Välj statsråd" in #5b6779 fixes both.
- Cap banner before completion scrolls away; the sticky bar should carry "M/KD-platser kvar: n".
- color-scheme: light with no dark ground declared; iOS dark mode can flash white behind the navy header.

## Questions to Consider
