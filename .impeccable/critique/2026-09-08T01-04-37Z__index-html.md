## Design Specificity Verdict

**Design review:** the data layer is now authored. Gold means SD and only SD, every grid and the meter sort SD first so "how much gold" reads instantly, the floor line at 12, the heavy-departments sentence, Archivo Black numerals. The chrome around it is still stock: split hero, white toolbar with pills, admin cards in an auto-fit grid, bottom sheet with search and chips. The first viewport shows 23 empty outlines that read as a form to fill in rather than a warning.

**Deterministic scan:** 2 findings on the static and rendered-initial pages, 1 on the completed page, all false positives. "helvetica" is the fourth fallback in an intentional system stack behind the self-hosted display face. "flat-type-hierarchy" fires because the static cascade cannot resolve the clamp() on the h1; measured, the h1 is 34px on phone and 56px on desktop against 12px meta text, a 2.8:1 spread. The previous run's 23 contrast findings and the em-dash advisory are gone: 0 uppercase elements, 0 em-dashes in the initial DOM, and every measured text pair passes 4.5:1, including dark text on gold at 6.0:1.

**Detector-only catches the review missed:** the preview server on port 3000 predated the allowlist change and returned 404 for the font, so every http-based measurement rendered the fallback face (restarted during this run; file-based renders were unaffected). The empty-seat outline in the hero measures 2.5:1 against navy, under the 3:1 non-text floor.

**Visual overlays:** none. The Chrome extension is not connected; no overlay was injected and no live server started.

## Overall Impression

The argument is finally visible: the meter filling gold from the left toward a hard line, the result card with its gold numeral and named departments. What remains is the moment the argument should land and doesn't. The cap fires off-screen, the completion toast covers the headline it announces, and the page has no sender. Three fixes, all within reach before launch.

## What's Working

1. **Colour discipline in the data.** SD-first ordering in grids and meter, the floor line, gold-tinted SD cards. The one place the site argues visually rather than verbally.
2. **The desktop result card.** Archivo Black, gold numeral, 24 labelled tiles, and the note naming which departments SD takes. Best copy and best composition on the page.
3. **State engineering.** Inert regions, focus trap and return verified by the detector agent in both viewports, one-level undo, saved-state restore, described blocked rows, 16px search input.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] No sender anywhere.** Neither the page nor the share text says who is behind the site. Days before an election, an anonymous persuasion site is a credibility and disclosure liability, and the first question a journalist asks.
Fix: a sender line under the hero and in the footer ("En kampanj från Centerstudenter" or whatever the legal sender is), in the share text, and in the OG description.
Suggested command: $impeccable clarify

**[P0] The cap moment is invisible.** The banner is in normal flow between the sticky bar and the result; when the 11th non-SD pick lands the visitor is around 1700px down and focus returns to the card, so the campaign's punchline appears as greyed names and a red paragraph in the next sheet.
Fix: drop the banner; let the sticky bar change state (one gold line: "Taket är nått – alla lediga poster går till SD") and turn every remaining empty card into a gold "Bara SD kvar" affordance. In the sheet, preselect the SD tab.
Suggested command: $impeccable shape

**[P1] Completion collision on phone.** The toast sits over "12 av 24…" for up to seven seconds, the result card is not scrolled into view, the sticky bar grows 20px because "24 / 24 tillsatta" wraps, and the gold share buttons break the gold-means-SD rule.
Fix: scroll the card under the bar on completion, suppress the toast when the card is on screen, nowrap the status items in a fixed 3-column row, and make share buttons white on navy.
Suggested command: $impeccable polish

**[P1] Sticky bar too tall on phone.** 118px at start, 138px complete, 14 to 20 percent of the viewport plus a 73px fixed CTA, leaving about 460px of content on an iPhone SE.
Fix: single row on phone with counts drawn at the meter's ends; hide "Testa/Slumpa" once anything is filled.
Suggested command: $impeccable adapt

**[P2] Picker offers no relevance.** 67 alphabetical rows for "EU-minister" while the data knows who currently holds it and who speaks for the area.
Fix: a "Föreslagna" group at the top from current holder and matching roles, then "Alla". Also give phone result tiles their post labels so a screenshot is legible.
Suggested command: $impeccable shape

- Lede says "M–L–KD–SD-regering" while L is excluded by default; the status line hardcodes "M/KD" even with L on.
- "Testa ett troligt förslag" versus the footer's "ingen prognos": pick one register.

- Empty hero seat outline at 2.5:1 on navy; raise the border alpha to about .42 for 3:1.
- Result tiles are SD-first, so they no longer match department order below; fine for the argument, confusing for cross-reference.
